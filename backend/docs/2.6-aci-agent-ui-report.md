## 2. Integration Fixes & Root Causes Surfaced During Live Ablation

- **Pre-Merge Contract & Schema Alignment**: Updated legacy assertions in `backend/test/integration-24-glue.test.js` and `backend/test/integration-25-e2e.test.js` where `full_aci` now resolves to `trajectory_eviction_v26` (while `aci_25` remains pinned to `phase_watermark_v25`), and adjusted `budget.maxTokens` (`54,000`) in `backend/test/kernel/execution-reasoning.test.js` and Step 2 mock `inputTokens` (`10,100`) in `backend/test/context/aci-25.test.js` for the added `query_symbol_graph` schema overhead.
- **Contract Target Parsing Fix (`backend/src/kernel/contract-reasoning.js`)**: `collectRequestedSymbols` previously misclassified format examples in `SPEC.md` / `CONSTRAINTS.md` (such as backticked `file(line,col): severity code: msg` and `file:line(:col)` inside `aci-05`) as exported symbols, causing false `missingExportedSymbols` readiness vetoes in `DeterministicAciProbeAdapter`. Skipping backticked format syntax switched `aci-05` deterministic validation cleanly to `1/1` across all arms.
- **Three Live-Ablation DTOC v2.6 Root Causes Caught & Fixed Before Final Archive**:
  1. **Over-broad `contractFiles` shielding (`backend/src/kernel/agent-loop.js`)**: `AgentLoop` passed `requiredChangedFiles` and `taskContract.requiredFiles` (target `src/` files) into `contractFiles` during `compress()` (while omitting both `contractFiles` and `maxTotalTokens` from `inspectEpoch()`), making `isStrictDtocContractPath` shield target `src/` files from eviction and driving `eligibleCandidates` to `0`. Restricted `contractFiles` strictly to `taskContract.contractFilesOnDisk` matching `spec.md|contract.json|constraints.md|agents.md` and passed aligned `maxTotalTokens` + `contractFiles` to both `inspectEpoch` and `compress`.
  2. **Multi-line `SUPPORTING` slices mistaken for compact stubs (`backend/src/context/aci-engine.js`)**: `isAciStubText()` treated any text starting with `[ACI-REF ` as an already-offloaded stub, bypassing `SUPPORTING` extracts (~600 tokens) emitted by `PreInjectionAdmissionController`. Scoped `isAciStubText()` strictly to compact single-line `[ACI-REF ...]` and `[ACI-SKIP ...]` stubs, marked parsed `SUPPORTING` slices in `parseReadOrExpandMetadata()`, and preserved the original `artifact://` full-blob URI on eviction in `compressMessagesEpoch()`.
  3. **Premature pre-edit Tier 2 eviction & DTOC Supervisor overhead (`aci-engine.js`, `agent-loop.js`, `supervisor.js`)**: `phasePassed` previously satisfied `step >= 6 && unverifiedSrc === 0` before any mutations had occurred, prematurely evicting Tier 2 source reads right after exploration and forcing re-reads. Guarded Tier 2 pre-verification bypass behind `hasAnyMutations` or `usedCumulative >= 0.72 * maxTotalTokens`, skipped DTOC Supervisor LLM calls on deterministic `onlySuperseded` Tier 1 epochs, and raised `DEFAULT_ACI_SUPERVISOR_CONFIG.maxOutputTokens` to `2048` to eliminate 1200-token JSON truncation.
